# GhostWires release licensing policy

GhostWires v4.3.0 is open source under the MIT licence supplied in this release.
Copyright (c) 2026 Yasin Mohammed Miah. Third-party code, dependencies and data
remain subject to their own notices and licences.

## Each release carries its own terms

The licence shipped with a particular release applies to that release. This
policy does not promise that every future GhostWires release will be open source.
For releases whose rights they control, the copyright holder may choose different
terms, including a proprietary licence, and may stop publishing future source
code or offering downloads at any time. Any future change must be stated in the
new release's licence and release notes; version numbers alone do not change terms.

## Earlier open-source releases remain open source

Changing the terms of a later release, withdrawing a download, or making the
repository private does not revoke MIT permissions already granted for copies
of earlier MIT releases. Recipients may continue to use, modify and redistribute
those copies under their original terms, including keeping public forks, provided
they comply with the licence. There is no right to recall those permissions merely
because the project changes direction. This policy adds no restriction to MIT.

This release does not retrospectively declare every historical release MIT-licensed.
For an earlier release, inspect the licence or express grant provided with it.

## Contributions and future licensing

Contributors retain copyright unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.
Do not assume the project owns external contributions. Before including outside
work in a differently licensed future release, verify the rights granted by its
licence and comply with attribution and other obligations. Obtain separate written
permission where necessary. Do not remove third-party notices.

The MIT licence does not grant ownership of the GhostWires name, logos or trademarks.
A licence to source code does not grant access to the hosted service, private
infrastructure, accounts, credentials or third-party API services.

## Release checklist

- Include the applicable LICENSE and third-party notices in the release archive.
- Record the release version, source commit and licence in the release notes.
- Update package metadata and the app's release notice if the licence changes.
- Preserve the licence and notices applying to code retained from earlier releases.
